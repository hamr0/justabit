Hi Amr,

I read -03 of your Agent-Delegation profile. The three-layer split in Section 3 is one of the clearest framings I have seen. So is the line that "A limit that the model enforces on itself is not a limit".

A question about the top of the chain. Your profile shows that each hop only narrows what it received from the originating principal. When an auditor later asks why the principal granted that first link, and who in an organization was accountable for it, do you see that as out of scope? Or would you expect a reference from the chain to a separate decision record?

That record is what we work on. EAGF is an open candidate specification for recording which decision authorized an agent's action, who is accountable for it, and what evidence shows what happened. It does not define a credential format or a delegation chain, so it is meant to sit next to work like yours, not compete with it.

If it would help, I can send a short mapping from your layers (a) and (c) to EAGF's records.

EAGF's text, by David Luna, is draft-luna-eagf-agent-delegation-semantics-00, an individual Internet-Draft (work in progress), not a standard and not endorsed by the IETF:
https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-luna-eagf-agent-delegation-semantics/
